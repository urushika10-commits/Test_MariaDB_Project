------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Conclusion: zero-config TLS gives ENCRYPTION but not VERIFICATION, and does
 not REQUIRE TLS. The auto cert is self-signed and ephemeral, so a client has
 nothing to check it against - an impostor (section 12) is accepted, and a
 plaintext connection is accepted too. This project therefore adds its own CA,
 ssl_verify_cert + ssl_verify_identity, and require_secure_transport=ON
 (section 9). That is the gap between 'SSL on by default' and verified TLS.
